# Playable Lineage Turnarounds — v1

This review set translates the approved silhouette candidates into full-color
production turnarounds. It uses the selected `allegiance-lineages-v5` designs
and `cinematic-storybook-anime-v1.png` as the shared style authority.

weapon preference. The weapons shown do not limit any Lineage Variant's final
gameplay equipment access.

## Lineage design checks

- Humans: brave, readable faces; royal-blue travel clothing; equal protection
  and equipment treatment for both variants.
- Tidekin: gender-neutral amphibious presentation, turquoise skin, frog-and-fish
  facial anatomy, webbed hands and feet, and no added Human gender cues. Candidate
  05 is the smooth-headed coastal scout, 08 the sturdy fin-crested marsh explorer,
  and 09 the taller staff-bearing tide warden.
- Grove Centaurs: serious expressions, warm deep-brown skin, pointed ears, very
  long wavy hair, and practical asymmetric leather-and-bronze armor.
- Aeralith: female-only presentation, sharp facial structure, high ponytails,
  red/gold/blue-grey martial clothes, and readable flight membranes.
- Crag Trolls: male-only presentation, immense friendly blue builds, with M04
  younger/heavier and M10 older/bearded.
- Deep Goblins: male-only presentation, compact cave gear and deliberately odd,
  asymmetric faces that remain distinct between M03 and M10.
- Sunscour: warm-brown skin, severe desert-warrior expressions, crimson wraps,
  practical armor, and equivalent protection across male and female sheets.
- Rimeborn: original nonhuman frostling-inspired anatomy, icy blue-grey skin,
  broad dark noses, pale eyes, white hair, heavy expedition coats, and packs.

## Shared production template

- Compact child-friendly proportions around 3.5–4 heads tall
- Warm ivory studio background, matched scale, and stable baselines
- Clean dark contours, broad color shapes, subtle handmade texture, and soft
  cel-painted shading
- Consistent face, hair, costume, palette, equipment, and construction details
  across all four views in a row
- Large readable heads, hands, feet, weapons, and attachment points suitable for
  later sprite reduction
- Every Character visibly carries gameplay equipment in every view

## Tidekin presentation

Tidekin candidates 05, 08, and 09 are active review designs. They remain
gender-neutral rather than being deferred; any future decision to introduce
gender-specific Lineage Variants should be treated as a separate lore and anatomy
decision rather than inferred from these sheets.

## Generation

- Main mode: built-in image generation, `stylized-concept`
- Corrective mode: `precise-object-edit` for the male Grove Centaur proportion
  correction
- Style authority: `art-source/style-references/cinematic-storybook-anime-v1.png`
- Color authority: `art-source/style-references/allegiance-lineages-v5/`
- Shape authority: selected candidates from
  `art-source/concepts/lineage-silhouettes/v3/numbered/`
- Output: 1536x1024 RGB PNG

These are review concepts, not final sprite sheets. The next gate is to select
or combine one production design per Lineage Variant before creating expression,
equipment, animation, and pixel-scale sprite studies.
