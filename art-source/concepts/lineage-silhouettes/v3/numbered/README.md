# Numbered Silhouette Review

These overlays preserve the original `v3` sheets and add stable identifiers for selection. IDs `01–05` are the top row and `06–10` are the bottom row.

The weapon shown beside an ID is only a readability test. Selecting a silhouette does not restrict that body or gender to the pictured weapon.

Selections can combine traits. For example, `Grove Centaur male: body 01,
armor 04` is valid. The production turnaround will reconcile those choices into
one coherent design.

## Gender-variant rules

- Lineage Variant availability follows the table above; a single-gender Lineage
  is not missing a counterpart.
- Lineages with both variants receive distinct male and female production
  turnarounds.
- Both variants retain the same unmistakable Lineage anatomy and cultural grammar.
- All genders can equip every Weapon Family, Grip, Shield, Staff, Wand, and armor category.
- Avoid making the female variant merely smaller, less armored, or more decorative.
- Avoid making the male variant the unmarked default.
- Use face, body distribution, hair or crest, posture, and garment fit together to communicate variation.
- Nonhuman Lineages do not need to reproduce Human sexual dimorphism; their differences should arise from their own anatomy.
- Tidekin remain unresolved and receive no gender-specific turnaround until that
  decision is made.

If none of the selected options survives the turnaround review, mark that
Lineage Variant `new`; it will receive a targeted silhouette pass before sprite
production.
