# Playable Lineage Silhouettes — v3

This review set rebuilds all eight playable Lineages from the selected `allegiance-lineages-v5` direction. It supersedes the earlier silhouette passes for review but does not canonize individual character designs.

## Shared silhouette grammar

- Child-friendly compact bodies around 3.5–4 heads tall, with large readable heads, hands, and feet
- Landscape 1536x1024 sheets with exactly two rows of five right-facing characters
- Warm ivory background, pale baselines, black filled shapes, and restrained ivory contour separations
- Simple clothing and broad outer contours rather than detailed JRPG armor
- Consistent camera, perceived scale, spacing, weapon proportions, and equipment order
- Every character visibly carries gameplay equipment
- Shields pair only with One-Handed Weapons; Two-Handed Weapons occupy both hands; no dual wielding

## Selected Lineage cues

- **Tidekin:** round amphibious heads, large circular eyes, strong thighs, webbed extremities, and occasional fins.
- **Humans:** brave profiles, simple hooded capes, tunics, belts, and practical boots.
- **Grove Centaurs:** integrated equine bodies, serious pointed-ear profiles, very long hair, and restrained asymmetric protection.
- **Aeralith:** sharp profiles, high wind-pulled ponytails, martial layers, directional fabric, and small gliding membranes.
- **Crag Trolls:** friendly monumental bodies with broad torsos, long arms, enormous hands, and simple slab clothing.
- **Deep Goblins:** large ears, raised goggles, highly varied strange faces, hooked noses, uneven eyes, and functional packs.
- **Sunscour:** layered robes, lamellar shoulder capes, wrapped limbs, head cloth, and lower-face veils.
- **Rimeborn:** nonhuman round eyes and broad faces, wiry hair, floppy fur hoods, thick coats, boots, and shelter packs.
