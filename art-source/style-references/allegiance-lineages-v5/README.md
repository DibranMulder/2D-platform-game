# Allegiance Lineage Style References — v5

This is the selected paired review set for all eight playable Lineages. Both plates retain the approved child-friendly storybook rendering while incorporating the user-provided visual references listed below.

## Shared rendering direction

- Whimsical 2D side-scrolling children's fantasy with only a light anime influence
- Compact characters around 3.5–4 heads tall, with large readable heads, hands, and feet
- Simplified clothing, broad color blocks, restrained ornament, and visible gameplay equipment
- Clean dark contours, soft cel-painted shading, and large layered fairy-tale environments
- Characters read as small adventurers inside a wondrous world rather than tall anime party-poster heroes

## Light Lineages

- **Tidekin:** round, curious amphibious adventurer with Spear and Shield.
- **Human:** brave, warm and focused adventurer with One-Handed Sword and Shield.
- **Grove Centaur:** serious guardian expression, warm deep-brown skin, angular cheeks, subtly pointed ears, very long loose wavy hair, broader upper body, and restrained asymmetric leather-and-bronze protection. The integrated equine body and Bow remain. Broad visual cues came from `../../concepts/lineage-silhouettes/ref/centaur.png`.
- **Aeralith:** sharp youthful face, keen eyes, high wind-pulled ponytail, poised open-hand air stance, and simple flowing ember-red, sunrise-gold, pale-blue, and blue-grey martial layers. The Staff and subtle gliding membranes remain. Broad movement and garment cues came from `../../concepts/lineage-silhouettes/ref/airbender.png`.

## Dark Lineages

- **Crag Troll:** friendly monumental mountain builder with Two-Handed Axe.
- **Deep Goblin:** strange, asymmetric face with large ears, uneven yellow eyes, long angular nose, pronounced cheeks, and raised goggles. The Crossbow remains. Broad facial cues came from `../../concepts/lineage-silhouettes/ref/goblin.png`.
- **Sunscour:** layered weathered dark-red desert robes, indigo under-layer, practical lamellar shoulder cape, wrapped forearms, head wrap, and lower-face veil. The Spear and Shield remain. Broad costume cues came from `../../concepts/lineage-silhouettes/ref/haradrim.png`.
- **Rimeborn:** distinctly nonhuman frost-adapted anatomy with slate skin, large reflective ice-blue eyes, broad nose, pale wiry facial hair, and oversized fur hood. The Staff remains. Broad anatomical cues came from `../../concepts/lineage-silhouettes/ref/frostling.png`.

The supporting references inform broad visual traits only. Exact characters, costumes, armor patterns, markings, symbols, weapons, and poses are not copied.

## Generation

- Mode: built-in image generation, `precise-object-edit`
- Art-style authority: `../cinematic-storybook-anime-v1.png`
- Supporting trait references: Centaur, air-warrior, Goblin, Haradrim, and Frostling images listed above
- Output: 1536x1024 PNG
