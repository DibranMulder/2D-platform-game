# Allegiance Lineage Style References — v1

These paired full-color plates apply the approved `cinematic-storybook-anime-v1.png` direction to all eight playable Lineages. They are group-level visual references, not final character designs.

## Light Lineages

`light-lineages.png`, from left to right:

1. Tidekin — Spear + Shield
2. Human — One-Handed Sword + Shield
3. Grove Centaur — Bow
4. Aeralith — Staff

The shared backdrop combines the Sea, Open Lands, Elder Forests, and Sky Reaches at a luminous dawn.

## Dark Lineages

`dark-lineages.png`, from left to right:

1. Crag Troll — Two-Handed Axe
2. Deep Goblin — Crossbow
3. Sunscour — Spear + Shield
4. Rimeborn — Staff

The shared backdrop combines the Broken Mountains, Underdeep, Ember Desert, and Ice Lands at twilight. Dark is presented as a cosmic-political Allegiance rather than a shorthand for evil or grotesque design.

## Shared generation direction

- Built-in image generation, `stylized-concept` mode
- Direct style reference: `../cinematic-storybook-anime-v1.png`
- Full-body four-character landscape group portraits at 1536x1024
- Graceful storybook-anime proportions, fine ink-like contours, controlled cel shading, and watercolor-and-gouache texture
- Every representative visibly carries gameplay equipment
- Original designs without copied characters, costumes, or faction symbols

The Dark plate additionally used the completed Light plate as a composition reference so scale, horizon, polish, and ensemble rhythm remain compatible across the pair.
