# Allegiance Lineage Style References — v3

This is the current paired review set. It retains the child-friendly side-scrolling storybook direction established in v2 and sharpens the facial identity of the four Light Lineages.

## Light facial language

- **Tidekin:** the roundest design, with large circular eyes and a curious, optimistic expression.
- **Human:** a broader childlike face, strong simple brows, focused eyes, a determined closed mouth, and a slightly raised chin. The read is brave and warm rather than angry.
- **Grove Centaur:** calm narrowed eyes, straight lowered brows, and a serious unsmiling guardian expression. Long, thick hair extending well below the shoulders is a required Lineage feature.
- **Aeralith:** the sharpest facial geometry in the Light roster, using a narrower face, small pointed chin, angled brows, keen almond-shaped eyes, and a subtle pointed nose while retaining compact child-friendly proportions.

The Light environment, composition, bodies, clothing, colors, and gameplay equipment remain based on the approved v2 direction. The Dark plate is carried forward unchanged from v2.
