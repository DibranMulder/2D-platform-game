# Launch Combat Class Talent Trees

## Tree structure

Each of the six starting Combat Classes has three branches with six sequential
Talents per branch: **18 Talents per Class and 108 Talents total**. The branches
change how a Class plays rather than merely adding percentages to the same
rotation.

The working unlock rows are Adventure Levels 10, 30, 50, 70, 90, and 110. A
Hero earns one Class Talent Point every ten Adventure Levels, reaching twelve
points at level 120. A Talent costs one point and requires the Talent immediately
to its left in the same branch. At the cap, a Hero may complete two branches or
build a mixed specialization; one Hero cannot own all eighteen Talents without
a future source of bonus points.

Baseline Class actions are learned outside the tree. A Duelist can Dual Wield
before buying a Talent, for example, and a Warden can cast both Radiance and
Gloam spells before specializing.

The exact point economy, respecialization rules, numerical values, cooldowns,
and PvP adjustments remain tuning decisions. Names and play patterns are the
current curation target.

## Vanguard

The Vanguard is the only launch Class centered on Weapon-and-Shield combat.
Bulwark absorbs pressure, Standard Bearer protects a group, and Weaponmaster
turns defense into deliberate counterattacks.

| Branch | Level 10 | Level 30 | Level 50 | Level 70 | Level 90 | Level 110 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bulwark | **Brace the Line** — Guard costs less Stamina and resists forced movement | **Shield Wake** — a guarded hit empowers the next Shield strike with a short shockwave | **Interpose** — rush to an ally and receive their next direct hit | **Unbroken Guard** — a Perfect Guard restores Stamina and Guard stability | **Bastion Field** — allies immediately behind the Vanguard take less frontal ranged damage | **Living Fortress** — enter a slow fortified stance with immense Guard and protective pulses |
| Standard Bearer | **Rallying Cry** — briefly improve nearby allies' movement and Guard recovery | **Claim the Ground** — plant a banner that strengthens allies inside its area | **Shared Resolve** — protecting a wounded ally grants both Heroes temporary Defense | **Formation Step** — moving near allies gives the group slow-resistance and stability | **Last Line** — the banner pulses a shield when an ally first falls below critical health | **Crowned Vanguard** — the banner follows the Vanguard and strengthens every formation effect |
| Weaponmaster | **Measured Counter** — the first attack after a Perfect Guard deals bonus stagger | **Sweeping Reprisal** — a counterattack may strike enemies on both sides | **Hampering Blow** — a precise hit reduces enemy attack speed instead of unequipping gear | **Iron Tempo** — alternating Guard and attack steadily accelerates recovery | **Judgment Counter** — countering a telegraphed Elite action exposes its weakness | **Perfect Retort** — a Perfect Guard may immediately reset one counter action |

## Ravager

The Ravager commits to Two-Handed Weapons. Fury rewards sustained aggression,
Breaker destroys defenses and terrain objects, and Colossus emphasizes reach,
weight, and deliberate battlefield control.

| Branch | Level 10 | Level 30 | Level 50 | Level 70 | Level 90 | Level 110 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fury | **Rising Heat** — consecutive committed hits build temporary attack speed | **Relentless Advance** — heavy attacks resist minor interruption while moving forward | **Pain into Power** — taking damage empowers the next fully charged swing | **Fury Unbound** — spending maximum Heat grants a brief burst of movement and recovery | **No Surrender** — once per encounter, a would-be stagger instead fills Heat | **Heart of Thunder** — maximum Heat transforms heavy attacks into chaining thunderous impacts |
| Breaker | **Splinter Armor** — charged hits apply a stacking Defense reduction | **Crushing Arc** — downward attacks damage shields, constructs, and breakable terrain more strongly | **Faultline** — a ground strike sends a short rupture along the platform | **Sundered Guard** — breaking Guard briefly prevents its full recovery | **Ruinous Impact** — striking an exposed Elite creates a party-wide damage opening | **Worldbreaker** — the next charged attack creates three sequential faultline eruptions |
| Colossus | **Heavy Hands** — Two-Handed Weapon reach and stagger increase slightly | **Anchored Swing** — charging an attack resists pull and knockback | **Giant's Reach** — the final strike of a combo gains a much wider arc | **Aftershock** — fully charged hits repeat a weaker impact after a delay | **Mountain Step** — a short armored step passes through lesser creatures | **Titan Form** — temporarily enlarge weapon arcs, stability, and environmental break power |

## Ranger

The Ranger supports Bow, Crossbow, and Spear play. Marksman is precision ranged
damage, Pathfinder is movement and trap control, and Wildbond creates a
relationship with one benevolent or neutral creature companion.

| Branch | Level 10 | Level 30 | Level 50 | Level 70 | Level 90 | Level 110 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Marksman | **Patient Aim** — remaining still briefly improves the next projectile's precision | **Piercing Shot** — a charged projectile passes through one additional target | **Marked Weakness** — hitting a telegraphed weak point marks it for the party | **Trueflight** — long-range attacks lose less damage and accuracy | **Thread the Needle** — striking through a small opening guarantees a critical weak-point hit | **Horizon Shot** — fire a screen-spanning shot that gains power for every marked target crossed |
| Pathfinder | **Quick Vault** — vaulting over a creature reloads or readies the next ranged attack | **Snareline** — place a visible line trap that roots the first lesser creature crossing it | **Smokeleaf Roll** — a dodge leaves a brief concealment cloud | **High Ground** — attacks from above gain stagger and trap damage | **Trail Without Trace** — movement no longer triggers ordinary environmental snares | **Every Road Is Mine** — deploy two traps at once and gain a reset when either is used cleverly |
| Wildbond | **Gentle Approach** — calm eligible neutral creatures and begin Companion bonds | **Companion Call** — summon one bonded creature with a simple support action | **Shared Senses** — the Companion reveals nearby tracks, Hidden Zone clues, and weak points | **Protective Instinct** — Hero and Companion shield one another when either is threatened | **Pack Tactics** — marked targets take coordinated attacks from both partners | **Legendary Bond** — unlock the Companion's signature action and prevent its first defeat each encounter |

Wildbond never turns every creature into a collectible pet. Only specifically
authored benevolent or neutral Companion candidates participate, and they are
befriended through interaction rather than ordinary loot drops.

## Duelist

The Duelist always uses two permitted One-Handed Weapons. Twin Dance develops
alternating-weapon combinations, Footwork focuses on position and evasion, and
Trickery manipulates enemy timing without becoming a stealth-only Class.

| Branch | Level 10 | Level 30 | Level 50 | Level 70 | Level 90 | Level 110 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Twin Dance | **Crosscut** — alternating Main Hand and Off Hand attacks adds a crossing finisher | **Opposed Edges** — using two different Weapon Families grants both a small signature bonus | **Twin Parry** — a precisely timed paired block can answer attacks from either side | **Flowing Combination** — switching hands extends the combo instead of restarting it | **Mirror Blades** — a critical strike empowers the opposite hand's next action | **Hundred-Step Finale** — unleash a rapid finisher whose pattern reflects the equipped pair |
| Footwork | **Quickstep** — gain a short low-cooldown evasive step | **Passing Cut** — Quickstep through an enemy to deliver a light crossing strike | **Perfect Distance** — attacks at the edge of weapon reach gain precision | **Airborne Feint** — redirect once in the air and conceal the landing direction | **Untouchable Rhythm** — successive near-misses shorten Quickstep recovery | **Dance Between Raindrops** — briefly chain Quicksteps while every successful evade empowers the finale |
| Trickery | **False Opening** — intentionally delay a strike to provoke an early enemy response | **Pommel Trick** — a close interruption deals high stagger but low damage | **Bleeding Nick** — precision hits apply a short non-stacking bleed | **Disarming Flourish** — reduce a creature's damage or suppress one weapon action; never steals player gear | **Final Feint** — once per combo, cancel a finisher into the opposite hand | **Curtain Call** — marked feints collapse at once, repeating part of the damage they enabled |

The Off-Hand Weapon is real Equipment, not a visual duplicate. Its Weapon
Family, stats, requirements, effects, and appearance all remain active. A
Duelist cannot equip a Shield or any Two-Handed Weapon while Dual Wielding.

## Arcanist

The Arcanist is the elemental mage. Embercraft specializes in damage and
burning zones, Frostweaving controls space and protects through ice, and
Stormscript chains attacks with aerial movement.

| Branch | Level 10 | Level 30 | Level 50 | Level 70 | Level 90 | Level 110 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Embercraft | **Kindle** — repeated fire hits build a small Burning effect | **Searing Sigil** — place a delayed fire rune on terrain | **Flame Step** — dash through a short flame path without taking contact damage | **Hungry Flame** — fire zones grow when they consume another elemental effect | **Phoenix Script** — once per encounter, critical health triggers a fiery escape and shield | **Living Inferno** — combine active fire zones into a moving elemental storm |
| Frostweaving | **Rime Dart** — frost projectiles progressively slow lesser creatures | **Crystal Ward** — form a breakable ice barrier on a platform | **Freeze Line** — draw a short freezing path across the ground | **Shattercraft** — breaking the Arcanist's ice effects launches safe fragments outward | **Winter Room** — create a large zone that slows actions and projectiles | **Still World** — briefly freeze lesser creatures and suspend Elite telegraphs without cancelling them |
| Stormscript | **Spark Chain** — lightning jumps once to a nearby marked target | **Gale Lift** — create an updraft for the Arcanist and allies | **Thunder Mark** — delayed lightning strikes a target after an obvious count | **Blink Current** — teleport a short distance along an active lightning connection | **Tempest Crown** — orbiting sparks answer nearby spell casts | **Skybreak** — call a storm column that repeatedly lifts, chains, and grounds targets |

## Warden

The Warden is a polarity mage, not a nature warrior. Radiance heals, shields,
and reveals; Gloam curses, conceals, and transfers vitality; Eclipse rewards
using both schools in deliberate sequence. These schools are independent of
Light and Dark Allegiance, and every Warden may learn both.

| Branch | Level 10 | Level 30 | Level 50 | Level 70 | Level 90 | Level 110 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Radiance | **Kindle Light** — healing or shielding leaves a small lingering glow | **Mending Ray** — channel a beam that heals an ally or harms a corrupted creature | **Halo Ward** — place a circular barrier that absorbs one heavy hit | **Revealing Flare** — expose concealed creatures, traps, and Gloam effects | **Sanctuary** — create a zone of healing and control resistance | **Second Sunrise** — once per encounter, restore a recently defeated ally with partial health |
| Gloam | **Gloam Bolt** — damaging spells leave a short shadow vulnerability | **Withering Mark** — reduce a target's outgoing damage and healing | **Shadebind** — pin a lesser creature's shadow to the ground | **Life Siphon** — transfer a portion of damage dealt into healing for the weakest nearby ally | **Night Mantle** — cloak the party from ordinary creature detection until they attack | **Long Eclipse** — spread active Gloam marks and extend their final tick |
| Eclipse | **Twilight Attunement** — alternating Radiance and Gloam reduces the second spell's cost | **Balanced Weave** — each school strengthens the next spell of the other school | **Dusk Exchange** — move one removable harmful effect from an ally onto a valid enemy | **Convergence** — overlapping Radiance and Gloam zones create a Twilight field | **Equinox** — keeping both Attunements balanced improves cast speed and stability | **Eclipse Incarnate** — temporarily let every Warden spell count as both schools for interactions |

Radiance is not morally pure and Gloam is not morally corrupt. A hostile Warden
may weaponize Radiance, while a benevolent Warden may use Gloam to hide refugees
or weaken a dangerous creature.

## Equipment and art implications

- Vanguard Talents may require a Shield and must visibly read through the
  Shield layer.
- Duelist attacks need independent Main Hand and Off Hand equipment layers,
  mirrored attachment points, and pair-aware animations.
- Ranger Wildbond requires a separate Companion entity and cannot be faked as a
  cosmetic pet.
- Arcanist and Warden may share Wand and Staff base animations, but their
  effects, casting poses, palettes, and armor silhouettes must remain distinct.
- Class Talents never decide Cape appearance; Capes remain generic equipment.
- Every spell and attack still needs authoritative server validation before
  multiplayer implementation.

## Existing prototype migration

The current sword prototype has Blade, Guardian, and Wayfarer branches with four
nodes each. It remains useful as a UI scaffold but is not the Vanguard tree.
The production tree UI must support six rows per branch, Class switching, level
requirements through 110, twelve spendable points, and Class-specific preview
copy.

The current equipment runtime also accepts only Shields in Off Hand. Duelist
Dual Wield therefore requires an explicit Class-aware equipment rule before the
tree can become playable.

## Curation questions

1. Should Ranger Wildbond remain a full branch or become a separate Companion
   system available to more Classes?
2. Should a Warden choose Radiance or Gloam permanently, or always retain access
   to both as proposed?
3. Are twelve Talent Points at level 120 restrictive enough to make builds
   distinct?
4. Should capstone Talents be active ultimate actions, passive transformations,
   or the mix proposed here?
